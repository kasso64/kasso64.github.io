The rewrite uses only facts from your pitch. When something is missing, you get a blank to fill, not an invented number.

How it works

  1. Paste or record

    Text in English, Russian or Kazakh, or an audio or video file up to 3 minutes.

  2. Get a score out of 16

    Seven checks: the metric, the customer pain, the US link, the team, the ask. Each one points to a quote from your own text.

  3. Take the US version

    An English rewrite with your best number first, plus six questions an investor is likely to ask.

Why you can trust the score

Every point has evidence
The AI must quote your pitch. Code checks the quote exists and decides the grade.
No invented facts
Numbers in the rewrite are checked against your original. Missing facts become blanks.
Nothing is stored
Your text and recording are used for the analysis and then dropped. No account, no database.
Made for Central Eurasia
Built for founders from Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and the region. Feedback in English or Russian; Kazakh in beta.
